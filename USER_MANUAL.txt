                         1. GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

1.1 Creating an Account
-----------------------
Note: In the current system, accounts are created by the admin. 
Contact your cafe staff to create an account for you.

Your account will include:
- Your name
- Email address (used for login)
- Password
- Customer role (allows ordering)

1.2 Logging In
--------------
Step 1: Open your web browser
Step 2: Navigate to: http://localhost:8000
Step 3: You will see the login page

        ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
        │    ☕ Cafe Management           │
        │    Sign in to your account      │
        ├─────────────────────────────────┤
        │    Email: [____________]        │
        │    Password: [____________]     │
        │                                 │
        │    [    Sign In    ]            │
        └─────────────────────────────────┘

Step 4: Enter your email and password
Step 5: Click "Sign In"
Step 6: You will be redirected to the menu page

1.3 Understanding the Dashboard
-------------------------------
After logging in, you will see:

Top Navigation Bar:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ☕ Cafe Management                                          │
│                                                             │
│  [Order] [My Orders]        👤 Your Name [User] [Logout]   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Navigation Links:
- Order → Browse menu and place new orders
- My Orders → View your order history and status

-----------------------

================================================================================
                         3. PLACING AN ORDER
================================================================================

3.1 Adding Items to Cart
------------------------
Method 1: Quick Add
- Click the [Add] button on any product
- Item is added to your cart with quantity 1

Method 2: Multiple Items
- Click [Add] multiple times to increase quantity
- Or adjust quantity in the cart panel

Visual Feedback:
- Cart button shows item count: 🛒 Cart (3)
- Cart panel slides in from the right

Click the "Cart" button to open the cart panel:

- Increase quantity: Click [+]
- Decrease quantity: Click [-]

- See running total
- Select order type
- Add special notes

3.3 Order Types
---------------
Dine-in:
- You will eat at the cafe
- Order will be served to your table
- Select if you're staying at the cafe

Takeaway:
- You will take the order to go
- Packaged for carrying
- Select if you're taking away

3.4 Checkout Process
--------------------
Step 1: Review your cart items
Step 2: Verify quantities are correct
Step 3: Select order type (Dine-in/Takeaway)
Step 4: Add any special notes (optional)
        Examples:
        - "Extra hot please"
        - "Less sugar"
        - "Table 5"
        - "Allergy: no nuts"

Step 5: Click "Place Order"
Step 6: Wait for confirmation

3.5 Order Confirmation
----------------------
After placing your order, you will see:

What happens next:
1. Order is sent to the kitchen
2. Barista receives the order
3. Order status changes to "Preparing"
4. When ready, status changes to "Completed"
5. You can collect your order

4.2 Understanding Order Status
------------------------------

🟡 PENDING
- Order received but not yet started
- You can cancel at this stage
- Barista will start preparing soon

🔵 PREPARING
- Barista is making your order
- Cannot cancel at this stage
- Please wait for completion

🟢 COMPLETED
- Your order is ready!
- Collect your order from the counter
- Enjoy your meal!

🔴 CANCELLED
- Order was cancelled
- By you or by staff
- No charge applied

4.4 Cancelling an Order
-----------------------
You can cancel orders that are still "Pending":

Step 1: Go to "My Orders"
Step 2: Find the pending order
Step 3: Click "Details"
Step 4: Click "Cancel Order" (red button)
Step 5: Confirm cancellation

Note: Cannot cancel if status is "Preparing" or "Completed"

================================================================================
                         6. TIPS & TRICKS
================================================================================

💡 Quick Tips:
--------------
1. Save Time: Remember your favorite orders for quick reordering
2. Special Requests: Use notes for allergies or preferences
3. Order Ahead: Place order before arriving for quick pickup
4. Check Status: Monitor order status while waiting
5. Group Orders: Add multiple items before checkout

🎯 Best Practices:
------------------
1. Review cart before placing order
2. Double-check order type (dine-in vs takeaway)
3. Add clear special instructions
4. Note your order number for pickup
5. Cancel promptly if plans change

⚠️ Important Notes:
-------------------
- Prices shown are final (include taxes)
- Orders cannot be modified after placement
- Cancel only pending orders
- Completed orders cannot be refunded through system
- Contact staff for issues with orders

================================================================================
                         7. FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)
================================================================================

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Ask the cafe staff to create an account for you. Provide your name 
   and email address.

Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A: No, but you can cancel pending orders and place a new one.

Q: How long does it take to prepare an order?
A: Typically 5-10 minutes for drinks, 10-15 minutes for food items.

Q: Can I order for someone else?
A: Yes, you can place orders for anyone. Just specify in notes.

Q: What if I forget my password?
A: Contact the cafe admin to reset your password.

Q: Can I save my favorite orders?
A: Currently, you need to manually add items each time. This feature 
   may be added in future updates.

Q: Is there a minimum order amount?
A: No minimum order. You can order a single item.

Q: Can I schedule an order for later?
A: Currently, all orders are for immediate preparation. Scheduling 
   may be added in future updates.

Q: How do I pay?
A: Payment is handled at the counter when you collect your order.

Q: Can I order from outside the cafe?
A: Yes, you can place orders remotely and pick up as takeaway.

================================================================================
                         8. GETTING HELP
================================================================================

If you need assistance:

1. Ask Cafe Staff
   - Show them your order number
   - Explain the issue
   - They can check order status

2. Contact Admin
   - For account issues
   - For technical problems
   - For feedback or suggestions

3. Common Issues & Solutions:

   Problem: Can't login
   Solution: Verify email and password, contact admin for reset

   Problem: Order not showing
   Solution: Refresh the page, check "My Orders" section

   Problem: Wrong items in order
   Solution: Contact staff immediately with order number

   Problem: Order taking too long
   Solution: Check order status, ask staff if delayed
